# Avoid

Words: delve, leverage, harness, foster, bolster, underscore, unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, streamline, utilise, robust, seamless, comprehensive, pivotal, crucial, innovative, cutting-edge, transformative, holistic, synergy, landscape, realm, testament, paradigm, nuanced. Use "is", not "serves as".

Minimisers: obviously, simply, just, of course, as you know.

Phrases: "It's worth noting", "It's important to note", "When it comes to", "At its core", "Here's the thing", "Let's break it down", "This is where X comes in", "Plays a crucial role", "In other words", "To summarise", "Going forward", "Unfortunately", "Great question", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if you have any questions".

Structures: "It's not just X, it's Y"; "No X. No Y. Just Z."; a quotable closing line; two clauses with the same shape; ranking your own points ("most importantly"); agreeing, then promising more; dramatic headings; announcing what you are about to say.
